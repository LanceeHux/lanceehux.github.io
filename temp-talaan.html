Pillar 01 • Market Strategy

Marketing Plan

Connecting our prototype's core value proposition to the right target audience through positioning, pricing, and promotion.

01 / Definition

Marketing Plan

A Marketing Plan is a strategic roadmap that outlines how a venture identifies its ideal customers, communicates the unique value of its prototype, and drives product adoption and sales.

"Core Goal: Turn target market awareness into measurable demand and loyal early adopters."

02 / Vocabulary

Key Concepts & Terms

5

Target Market & Segmentation

Dividing a broad consumer base into specific demographic, geographic, and behavioral groups most likely to buy.

Marketing Mix (The 4Ps)

Balancing Product design, Price strategy, Place (distribution channels), and Promotion tactics.

Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

The distinct functional or emotional advantage that sets our prototype apart from existing competitors.

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Total marketing and sales expense required to convert a single prospective lead into a paying customer.

Pillar 02 • Execution & Workflow

Operational Plan

Translating the prototype blueprint into a repeatable, high-quality production and delivery system.

01 / Definition

Operational

An Operational Plan details the day-to-day processes, raw material sourcing, production workflows, equipment, and quality standards needed to build and deliver the product consistently.

"Core Goal: Maximize efficiency, minimize waste, and guarantee consistent prototype quality."

02 / Vocabulary

Key Concepts & Terms

4

Supply Chain & Sourcing

The network of vendors, raw material suppliers, and logistics partners moving components from origin to customer.

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Step-by-step documented instructions ensuring every team member assembles and tests units identically.

Quality Assurance (QA)

Systematic inspection checkpoints that detect defects before a prototype reaches end users.

Just-In-Time (JIT) Inventory

Ordering components only as needed for active production batches to reduce storage costs and dead stock.

Pillar 03 • Viability & Profit

Financial Plan

Proving economic sustainability through unit economics, startup capital budgeting, and break-even analysis.

01 / Definition

Financial Plan

A Financial Plan is a quantitative projection of a venture's startup costs, pricing model, revenue streams, and expenses—demonstrating when the prototype becomes profitable and sustainable.

"Core Goal: Validate healthy unit margins, manage cash flow, and reach break-even rapidly."

02 / Vocabulary

Key Concepts & Terms

4

CAPEX vs. OPEX

Capital Expenditures (one-time tools/molds) vs. Operating Expenses (recurring materials, utilities, marketing).

Break-Even Point (BEP)

The exact sales volume where total revenue equals total fixed and variable costs (zero net loss or profit).

Gross & Net Profit Margin

Percentage of revenue retained after Cost of Goods Sold (Gross) and after all operating taxes/expenses (Net).

Return on Investment (ROI)

A ratio measuring net profit generated relative to the initial capital invested by founders or stakeholders.
